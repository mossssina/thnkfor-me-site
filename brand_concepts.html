— How to choose

AEditorial Intelligence

Serif meets system. Warm off-white paper, ink gold accents, Playfair italic display. Feels like a magazine that happens to be a marketing agency.

BEST FOR: Premium clients · design-led brands · founders who care about taste

BHuman × Machine

Built around the missing "I". Cursor as identity. Bold Space Grotesk, Signal Orange as the single accent. Concept-first, motion-first, memorable.

BEST FOR: Bold positioning · brand-first storytelling · press & industry recognition

COperating System

Marketing as a platform. Modular grid, live status indicators, agent cards. N36 fits naturally as a sub-brand. Feels like a product, not an agency.

BEST FOR: Product-led positioning · sub-brand roadmap · tech-adjacent audiences
